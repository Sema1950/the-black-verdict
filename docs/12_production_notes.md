## What We Keep

- Strong grimdark Inquisitor mood
- Mature, severe male face direction
- Full-body power armor presence
- Long cloak silhouette
- Dark palette with brass/gold accents
- Battle-used, ceremonial material feeling
- Standing authority pose
- High-ranking character presence

## What We Modify

- Cape and armor connection must be clarified
- Shoulder armor must stay human-scale
- Chest armor must avoid Space Marine proportions
- Rosette/authority object must be clearly designed
- Weapon placement must be cleaner and more functional
- Belt area must not become overloaded
- Cloak must not hide too much armor
- Hood should rest behind the head or upper back

## What We Reject

- Any fake readable text
- Random symbols
- Unverified iconography
- Impossible armor construction
- Confusing cape intersections
- Space Marine backpack or power unit silhouette
- Oversized Astartes-style pauldrons
- Excessive chains
- Too many skulls, seals, or relics

## Production Rule

Use the selected concept image for mood and visual direction only.

Do not copy it directly.

The final 3D design must be rebuilt from:
- Approved project documents
- Official references
- Real-world material references
- Clean 3D construction logic
