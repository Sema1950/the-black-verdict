# 03 Character Concept

## File Purpose

This file defines the working character concept before visual design, modeling, or technical production begins.

The goal is to lock the character logic first, so the final 3D character feels lore-faithful, believable, cinematic, and suitable for a professional portfolio.

This file should answer:

* Who is the character?
* What does he represent?
* What is his role inside the Inquisition?
* What should the viewer understand in the first 3 seconds?
* What design choices are allowed, limited, or not decided yet?

## Concept Status

Current status: revised and synchronized after power armor pivot.

This is not the final visual design bible.

This file defines the character direction. The next file, `04_visual_design_bible.md`, translates this concept into exact visual rules, shapes, materials, proportions, and asset decisions.

## Core Concept Summary

A human-scale, high-ranking Imperial Inquisitor with a serious, controlled, battle-hardened presence.

He is inspired by Henry Cavill’s strong facial structure and mature screen presence, but he should not be a direct commercial copy.

The character should read as:

* Powerful, but human
* Noble, but dangerous
* Religious, but not priest-like
* Militarized, but not a Space Marine
* Cinematic, but believable
* Detailed, but not overdecorated
* High-status, but still field-capable
* Expensive, battle-used, and ceremonial

The strongest current direction is:

A Puritan-in-philosophy, non-psyker Ordo Malleus Inquisitor wearing custom human-scale power armor, a long cloak, a hood resting behind the head or on the upper back, restrained gothic details, a controlled rosette, one power sword, one bolt pistol, limited purity seals, and one optional signature relic if it earns its place.

## One-Sentence Character Pitch

A disciplined Ordo Malleus Inquisitor in custom human-scale power armor, looking like a noble judge, battlefield veteran, and executioner of forbidden threats, carrying terrifying Imperial authority without becoming a Space Marine.

## Working Title

The Black Verdict.

Meaning:

The name suggests judgment, finality, authority, and severe consequence.

It is a working title only and can be changed later if the safer original portfolio version needs a less direct fan-art title.

## Design Pillars

### 1. Human Authority

The character must look like an elite human authority figure, not a genetically enhanced super-soldier.

Use:

* Tall realistic proportions
* Strong posture
* Controlled facial expression
* Human-scale custom power armor
* Long cloak silhouette
* Hood resting behind the head or on the upper back
* Practical weapon and belt layout

Avoid:

* Space Marine proportions
* Space Marine power armor silhouette
* Oversized pauldrons
* Astartes-style backpack or power unit silhouette
* Chapter-like markings
* Armor that hides the human body completely

### 2. Controlled Gothic Severity

The design should feel unmistakably grimdark and Imperial-inspired, but not randomly decorated.

Use:

* Restrained purity seals
* Tarnished metal
* Deep red wax
* Parchment only where justified
* Rosette or authority badge
* Gothic trim used with purpose
* Moderate gold or brass details
* Possible tabard or controlled chains after exploration

Avoid:

* Fake inscriptions
* Random Latin-looking text
* Too many skulls
* Too many chains
* Fantasy knight decoration
* Decoration without role or story
* Chaos-adjacent chain or skull language

### 3. Professional Portfolio Readability

The character must be readable as a high-end game character.

The final design should demonstrate:

* Likeness sculpting
* Mature male anatomy
* Hard-surface armor modeling
* Cloth construction
* Leather construction
* Material realism
* Texturing and wear control
* Game-ready real-time presentation quality

Every major asset should help the portfolio piece.

## Recommended Character Direction

### Preferred Ordo Direction

Final locked Ordo:

Ordo Malleus.

Reason:

Ordo Malleus gives the strongest cinematic daemon-hunter / exorcist direction. It supports Henry Cavill-inspired authority, warding discipline, custom power armor, relic logic, and strong portfolio value.

Warning:

The design must stay human-scale and controlled. Avoid fantasy excess, relic clutter, too many chains, too many skulls, or Space Marine proportions.

### Secondary Viable Direction

Ordo Hereticus as secondary reference only.

Reason:

Ordo Hereticus is not the final direction. It may still inform severity, discipline, judicial tone, and restraint.

Risk:

Do not let Hereticus elements pull the design toward priest, zealot, commissar, or Adepta Sororitas visual language.

### Direction Not Recommended As Default

Ordo Xenos.

Reason:

Ordo Xenos can be strong, but it risks becoming too generic sci-fi unless the Imperial gothic identity is very clear. It is better as a later alternate concept, not the main direction.

## Puritan / Radical Position

Locked direction:

Puritan in philosophy, severe in method.

Reason:

He follows strict Imperial doctrine. He may be ruthless, tired, and morally severe, but the design should stay readable as controlled Imperial authority.

Allowed ambiguity:

His ambiguity should be shown through expression, scars, field wear, material age, and controlled storytelling, not forbidden artifacts.

Avoid for v1:

* Daemonic objects
* Xenos trophies
* Chaos-looking relics
* Suspicious glowing artifacts
* Random occult symbols

## Psyker Decision

Current status: locked.

Decision:

Non-psyker.

Reason:

This keeps the design grounded, avoids fantasy effects, glowing eyes, and force-weapon complexity, and lets the portfolio focus on face, custom power armor, cloak, material realism, and controlled Ordo Malleus visual storytelling.

Non-psyker Malleus note:

Because he is non-psyker, the Ordo Malleus direction increases reliance on warding, sanctified equipment, rare armor, strict discipline, and approved relic logic.

This creates a risk of relic clutter.

The prop cap must remain strict.

## Rank And Status

Working rank direction:

Senior Inquisitor or Inquisitor Lord-level presence, but not overbuilt into a walking throne or parade monument.

He should feel important enough to command soldiers, interrogators, and agents, but still physically present in the field.

Visual implication:

* High-quality materials
* Rare equipment
* Custom power armor
* Authority badge or rosette
* Strong tailoring and armor fit
* Controlled ceremonial details
* Practical field wear

Avoid:

* Looking like a basic acolyte
* Looking like a normal Imperial Guard officer
* Looking like an Ecclesiarchy priest
* Looking like a Space Marine officer

## Personality

Core personality:

* Disciplined
* Severe
* Intelligent
* Controlled
* Tired but unbroken
* Dangerous without being loud
* Faithful, but not theatrical
* Capable of judgment and execution

Facial expression:

Calm, stern, and focused.

Not angry by default. Not heroic-smiling. Not insane. Not screaming.

The face should suggest that he has seen terrible things and still remains in control.

## Likeness Direction

The face may be inspired by Henry Cavill, especially:

* Strong jaw structure
* Broad masculine facial planes
* Heavy brow presence
* Balanced nose and mouth proportions
* Mature heroic skull shape
* Controlled serious expression
* Short beard or disciplined facial hair

Preferred final direction:

Close Henry Cavill-inspired likeness, older and more battle-worn than the actor’s usual heroic presentation, but still not a direct commercial replica.

Do not present the final character as an official Henry Cavill asset unless the project is clearly framed as private study or non-commercial fan art.

## Age And Physical Condition

Recommended apparent age:

Mid-to-late 40s.

Physical condition:

* Strong
* Athletic
* Broad-shouldered
* Realistic human build
* Not bodybuilder exaggerated
* Not Space Marine scale
* Battle-worn veteran
* Visible fatigue and scars

The body should support power armor, cloak, and weapon weight without looking superhuman.

## Silhouette Direction

Primary silhouette:

Human-scale custom power armor with long cloak.

Key silhouette elements:

* Bare head as the primary presentation choice
* Face as the hero asset
* Broad, powerful, but still human-scale armored shoulders
* No high collar as a dominant design feature
* Hood present, resting behind the head or on the upper back
* Long cloak over the armor
* Possible front and back tabard, pending visual exploration
* Possible controlled chain arrangement instead of or alongside tabard, pending visual exploration
* Rosette area as authority focal point
* Controlled weapon placement
* Heavy armored boots grounded and practical

No helmet in v1.

No wide-brimmed hat in v1.

No Space Marine backpack or power unit silhouette.

The silhouette should read as an Inquisitor in power armor before small details are visible.

## Costume Direction

Recommended costume structure:

* Custom human-scale power armor
* Long cloak over the armor
* Hood resting behind the head or on the upper back
* Possible front and back tabard, pending visual exploration
* Possible controlled chain arrangement, pending visual exploration
* Practical belt or integrated armor harness
* Armored gloves
* Heavy armored boots
* Restrained gothic metal trim
* Purity seals only in meaningful places

The armor must feel powerful, expensive, ceremonial, and battle-used, while still clearly belonging to a human Inquisitor rather than a Space Marine.

Avoid:

* Space Marine proportions
* Astartes-style backpack or power unit silhouette
* Oversized pauldrons
* Armor so large that it hides the human-scale body completely
* Random armor panels with no attachment logic
* Pure fantasy knight armor
* Excessive religious robes
* Overdecorated chain clutter
* Fake inscriptions

## Armor Direction

Recommended default:

Custom human-scale Inquisitorial power armor.

Reason:

The user prefers power armor as the main v1 direction. Power armor is lore-appropriate for some Inquisitors and gives the character stronger authority, heavier presence, and better Ordo Malleus visual impact.

Rules:

* Must remain human-scale
* Must not read as Space Marine armor
* No Astartes-style backpack or power unit silhouette
* No oversized pauldrons
* No chapter markings
* Face must remain visible and dominant
* Armor should look expensive, powerful, ceremonial, and battle-used
* Decoration must stay controlled

Previous carapace-over-coat direction:

Superseded for v1.

It may remain useful only as secondary reference for restraint, human scale, and material logic.

## Color Palette

Recommended palette:

* Black
* Dark charcoal
* Blackened gunmetal
* Deep red
* Dark brown leather
* Tarnished brass or aged gold
* Parchment
* Deep red wax
* Bone or ivory only if later approved for restrained detail

Color logic:

Dark, severe, and expensive-looking.

Gold should be aged and restrained. Red should support authority, wax seals, lining, and controlled accent detail, not turn the character into a superhero.

## Material Direction

Main material families:

* Mature skin
* Dark hair and beard groom
* Under-armor material
* Custom power armor
* Blackened gunmetal
* Tarnished brass or gold trim
* Heavy cloak cloth
* Aged black or brown leather
* Wax seals
* Aged parchment
* Optional chain metal
* Bone or ivory detail only if later approved
* Glass or lens material only if justified

Material storytelling:

The character should look used, maintained, field-worn, expensive, and ceremonial.

Not clean showroom armor. Not muddy wreckage. Controlled wear.

## Weapon Direction

Current status:

Primary structure approved.

Approved weapon structure:

* Primary melee weapon: power sword
* Secondary sidearm: bolt pistol
* Authority object: rosette
* Optional signature relic: pending
* No books in v1
* No daemonhammer in v1
* No inferno pistol in v1

Power sword rule:

The sword should be sheathed or held low in the main hero pose. It should not dominate the face, cloak, armor, and authority silhouette.

Bolt pistol rule:

The pistol should sit believably in a holster or controlled hand position. It should not become the main hero object.

Avoid:

* Multiple hero weapons
* Oversized fantasy weapon proportions
* Glowing weapon as default
* Force weapon logic because the character is non-psyker

## Signature Prop Direction

Mandatory authority object:

* Rosette or badge of authority

Optional signature relic, one maximum:

* No relic
* Warding reliquary
* Sealed document case or scroll tube
* Controlled chain arrangement as visual design element, not a separate relic

Books are not part of v1.

Current best recommendation:

Rosette plus no optional relic unless the silhouette needs stronger Ordo Malleus identity.

If a relic is needed, the best option is a restrained warding reliquary.

Do not approve multiple relics. Do not approve a servo-skull here.

## Rosette Direction

The rosette is the key authority symbol.

Recommended placement options:

* On chain
* On belt
* In a small chest badge case
* Partly hidden and revealed in final presentation close-up

Current recommendation:

Small chest badge case or chain placement for the power armor direction.

Avoid for now:

Huge superhero-style chest emblem.

Reason:

A controlled placement feels more believable and more mature.

Needs decision:

Final placement.

Needs IP review:

Fan-art symbol treatment versus safer original symbol treatment.

## Servo-Skull Decision

Current status:

No servo-skull in v1.

Recommendation:

Do not include it in the v1 asset checklist or concept scope.

Reason:

A servo-skull is lore-appropriate but outside the first production scope. It may be added later as a separate companion asset for v2 presentation.

## Books Decision

Current status:

No books in v1.

Reason:

The user does not want books for this version.

Additional reason:

Books increase text, inscription, and clutter risks.

This excludes:

* Relic book
* Chained tome
* Open book prop
* Book-as-hero-object

## Text And Inscription Direction

Default decision:

No readable text yet.

Purity seals, parchment, armor inscriptions, and document objects should use blank or unreadable placeholder detail until approved.

Do not use:

* Fake Latin
* Fake High Gothic
* Random prayers
* Copied online text
* Long official copyrighted passages

Readable text can be added later only after verification.

Current recommendation:

Avoid readable text in v1 or keep it as non-readable material noise.

## Tabard And Controlled Chain Direction

Status:

Pending visual exploration.

Possible directions:

### A. Cloak plus front/back tabard

More ceremonial and traditional.

Risk:

Can become fantasy knight or priest-like if overdone.

### B. Cloak plus controlled chain arrangement

More intimidating and heavy.

Risk:

Can become fantasy executioner, Chaos-adjacent, or visually cluttered.

Current recommendation.

Reason:

It preserves Inquisitorial ceremony while adding Ordo Malleus weight and intimidation, without letting chains dominate the design.

## Visual Storytelling

The character should feel like he has survived many investigations and campaigns.

Story should be shown through:

* Subtle scars
* Skin fatigue
* Worn leather edges
* Polished contact areas on metal
* Controlled dirt in seams
* Old but maintained armor
* Battle-used cloak hem
* Wax cracks
* Restrained seals
* Serious grooming

Avoid:

* Random blood
* Gore
* Excessive damage
* Cartoon villain styling
* Over-the-top heroic posing

## Presentation Pose Direction

Intended hero pose:

Standing authority pose.

The weapon should be sheathed or held low, not used in an action pose.

One hand may rest near the belt, rosette, cloak edge, or sword hilt.

Posture should be calm, severe, controlled, and judgmental.

The pose should show:

* Face
* Power armor silhouette
* Cloak silhouette
* Belt and rosette area
* Sword and pistol placement
* Material quality

Avoid:

* Screaming
* Running
* Attacking
* Exaggerated superhero posing
* Weapon blocking the face

### Safer Original Portfolio Version

This version is safer for commercial portfolio use.
