# 01 Lore Research

## File Purpose

This file collects the lore foundation for the Inquisitor character project.

The goal is not to create a full Warhammer encyclopedia. The goal is to control design decisions so the final character feels believable, lore-faithful, and not randomly decorated.

This file must support the current approved v1 direction:

A human-scale, non-psyker, Puritan-in-philosophy Ordo Malleus Inquisitor in custom human-scale Inquisitorial power armor.

## Research Status

Current status: revised and synchronized after power armor pivot.

This file now treats power armor as the approved main v1 armor direction, not an alternate concept.

Use this file as a living document. Update it whenever new lore, official references, visual rules, or design restrictions are verified.

## Main Lore Rule

Every visible design element must support the character’s role as an Inquisitor.

Avoid anything that is added only because it looks cool.

If the lore status of an item is uncertain, mark it as:

Needs lore verification.

## Source Priority

Use sources in this order:

1. Official Games Workshop material
2. Warhammer Community articles
3. Official Warhammer product pages and miniature references
4. Official rulebooks, codex books, and campaign books when available
5. Official licensed games and cinematics
6. Black Library novels for character tone and atmosphere
7. Lexicanum only as a secondary reference check
8. Fan wikis, Reddit, Pinterest, and ArtStation only as support or inspiration, not final lore authority

## Reference Use Warning

Do not treat every Inquisition-related miniature as equal reference.

Full Inquisitors, Interrogators, Acolytes, Agents, retainers, priests, soldiers, and specialists have different rank and visual language.

For this project, primary visual reference should come from actual Inquisitor-level characters and models.

Priority Inquisitor references to collect:

* Inquisitor Lord Torquemada Coteaz
* Inquisitor Greyfax
* Inquisitor Kyria Draxus
* Inquisitor Eisenhorn, with caveats
* Inquisitor Karamazov, with legacy / scale caution
* Classic Inquisitor 54mm models, with source verification

Use Inquisitorial Agents, Ashes of Faith, acolytes, and retinue models as secondary support only.

They are useful for props, scale, material language, and field equipment, but they should not define the main silhouette of a high-ranking Inquisitor.

## Eisenhorn And Ravenor Caveat

Eisenhorn and Ravenor are useful for tone, personality, and Inquisition atmosphere.

However, official visual material is limited compared with major tabletop miniatures.

Do not treat random online Eisenhorn or Ravenor images as canon.

Acceptable use:

* Official Black Library covers
* Official miniature or product images when available
* Official licensed artwork if verified

Everything else should be marked as fan interpretation.

## Core Inquisition Concept

The Inquisition is a powerful Imperial institution that investigates and destroys extreme threats to the Imperium.

Inquisitors are not normal soldiers. They are investigators, judges, commanders, interrogators, and executioners operating with exceptional authority.

Design implication:

The character should look like a human authority figure with military, religious, investigative, and ceremonial layers.

He should not look like a Space Marine, Imperial Guard officer, Rogue Trader, tech-priest, priest, or generic sci-fi knight.

## The Three Major Ordos

### Ordo Hereticus

Primary focus:

* Heresy
* Corruption inside humanity
* Rogue psykers
* Mutants
* Chaos cults
* Internal threats to Imperial faith and order

Design direction:

* Investigative and judicial
* Severe, disciplined, and puritanical
* Strong religious tone, but controlled
* Good for a character who hunts traitors, cultists, and corrupt Imperial officials
* Possible connection to Adepta Sororitas themes, but only with restraint

Visual ideas to verify:

* Seals of judgment
* Scroll cases and authority documents
* Condemnor boltgun
* Heavy coat with armor underneath
* Stern, uncompromising facial expression
* Interrogation-related props only if tasteful and non-graphic

Risk:

Can become too much like a priest, zealot, or witch-hunter if not balanced with sci-fi military authority.

The Adepta Sororitas connection is lore-correct, but visually dangerous for this project. Too much Sororitas-adjacent styling can make him read as ecclesiarchal rather than as a controlled, high-authority Inquisitor.

### Ordo Xenos

Primary focus:

* Alien threats
* Xenos infiltration
* Forbidden alien technology
* External non-human enemies

Design direction:

* Tactical, exploratory, and intelligence-focused
* More field equipment and scanning tools
* Possible containment devices or alien trophies, but only if lore-verified
* Good for a more practical, field-worn character
* Possible connection to Deathwatch themes, but avoid Space Marine visual language

Visual ideas to verify:

* Xenos containment canisters
* Tactical auspex or scanner device
* Exotic but controlled equipment
* Small alien trophy elements only if lore-appropriate
* Practical field-worn costume

Risk:

Can become too generic sci-fi if the gothic Imperial identity is weak.

### Ordo Malleus

Primary focus:

* Daemons
* Warp corruption
* Extreme supernatural threats
* Daemonic possession and influence

Design direction:

* Severe, secretive, and occult-resistant
* Strong relic, warding, purity, and exorcist feeling
* High visual intensity
* Good fit for a powerful cinematic Inquisitor character
* Strong option for a Henry Cavill-inspired authority figure

Visual ideas to verify:

* Warding seals
* Sanctified equipment
* Reliquary objects
* Power armor
* Heavy ceremonial armor
* Power sword
* Bolt pistol
* Controlled chain arrangements
* Black, deep red, parchment, tarnished gold, and sacred-metal accents

Risk:

Can become too fantasy or too overdesigned if we add too many relics, chains, skulls, and symbols.

## Current Best Ordo Choice For This Project

Locked direction:

Ordo Malleus.

Reason:

Ordo Malleus best supports the cinematic daemon-hunter and exorcist tone, Henry Cavill-inspired authority figure, warding discipline, heavy armor logic, and strong portfolio silhouette.

Ordo Hereticus remains useful as secondary reference for severity, judicial tone, and restraint, but it is not the main concept direction.

Ordo Xenos is not recommended for this character.

Warning:

Ordo Malleus must stay controlled and human-scale. Avoid fantasy excess, relic clutter, too many chains, too many skulls, or Space Marine proportions.

## Puritan vs Radical Design Implications

This decision must be made early because it changes the whole prop list, weapon logic, symbol logic, and silhouette.

### Puritan Inquisitor

A Puritan Inquisitor follows stricter Imperial doctrine and rejects forbidden tools.

Visual direction:

* Cleaner Imperial authority
* More formal and controlled silhouette
* Approved Imperial weapons and relics
* Stronger purity seal logic
* Fewer suspicious trophies
* Less experimental equipment
* Less visual ambiguity

Good fit for:

A serious, disciplined, Cavill-inspired authority figure.

### Radical Inquisitor

A Radical Inquisitor may use dangerous, forbidden, xenos, or daemon-related tools if they believe the result serves the Imperium.

Visual direction:

* Stranger relics or trophies
* Possible xenos artifacts
* Possible daemon-related containment items
* More morally ambiguous design
* More visual storytelling
* Higher lore risk

Risk:

A radical design can become visually messy or accidentally read as Chaos-corrupted if not controlled.

## Current Philosophy Direction

Locked direction:

Puritan in philosophy, severe in method.

Reason:

The character follows strict Imperial doctrine. He may appear ruthless, tired, scarred, and morally severe, but his ambiguity should come through expression, wear, scars, field damage, and controlled storytelling, not through forbidden artifacts.

Non-psyker Malleus tension:

Because the character is non-psyker, he relies more on warding, sanctified equipment, rare armor, disciplined training, and approved relic logic.

This increases the temptation to add too many props.

The prop cap must remain strict.

## Psyker Decision

Locked direction:

Non-psyker.

Reason:

This keeps the design grounded, avoids fantasy effects, glowing eyes, overt occult power, and force-weapon complexity.

The portfolio focus should stay on:

* Face and likeness quality
* Custom human-scale power armor
* Cloak and hood silhouette
* Hard-surface armor construction
* Material realism
* Controlled Ordo Malleus storytelling

Design implication:

He may hunt daemons, but he does not visually present as a wizard, sorcerer, or glowing psyker.

## Inquisitor Visual Identity

An Inquisitor can be highly individualized. There is no single uniform that every Inquisitor must wear.

However, the character should still clearly communicate:

* Imperial authority
* High rank
* Religious severity
* Suspicion and control
* Access to rare equipment
* Human scale and human anatomy
* Personal history and field experience

## Human Scale Rule

Do not make him look like a Space Marine.

Avoid:

* Oversized Space Marine proportions
* Massive pauldrons that make him read as Adeptus Astartes
* Chapter-specific markings
* Space Marine backpack silhouette
* Astartes-style power unit silhouette
* Space Marine power armor silhouette
* Barrel torso
* Tiny head / huge armor ratio
* Boots that visually shrink the body into Astartes scale

Preferred:

* Tall, powerful human body
* Strong but realistic anatomy
* Custom human-scale power armor
* Long cloak over armor
* Hood resting behind the head or on the upper back
* Practical armored boots and gloves
* Controlled belt, holster, scabbard, rosette, and limited seals

## Power Armor Decision

Power armor is approved as the main v1 armor direction for this character.

Some Inquisitors can wear power armor. Coteaz remains an important official reference for a heavily armored Inquisitor, but he should be used to understand what is allowed, not copied directly.

The character will wear custom human-scale Inquisitorial power armor.

The armor must not read as Space Marine armor.

Approved direction:

* Human-scale custom power armor
* No Space Marine proportions
* No Astartes-style backpack silhouette
* No visible power unit / backpack silhouette
* No oversized pauldrons
* No chapter markings
* Bare head as the primary presentation
* Strong, expensive, battle-used armor
* Black, deep red, and tarnished gold / brass palette
* Long cloak over the armor
* Hood present as part of the cloak or upper back cloth structure
* Front and back tabard or controlled chain arrangement remains pending visual exploration
* Moderate gothic decoration
* Limited purity seals
* No readable inscriptions until verified and approved

Design reason:

This direction gives the character stronger authority, more visual power, and a more imposing Ordo Malleus presence while still preserving the human-scale Inquisitor identity.

Primary risk:

Power armor can easily drift into Space Marine visual language.

Control rule:

Every armor shape must be checked against the human-scale rule. The silhouette should read as an Inquisitor in power armor, not a Space Marine without a helmet.

## Hero Prop Scope Rule

Keep the character controlled.

Maximum recommended hero props:

* One primary melee weapon
* One secondary sidearm
* One mandatory rosette or authority object
* One optional relic maximum
* Restrained seal and parchment work
* Optional tabard or controlled chain arrangement, pending visual exploration

Do not turn the design into a shopping list of every Inquisition object.

Every prop must earn its place through silhouette, lore, or character storytelling.

## Locked V1 Weapon Direction

Approved weapons:

* Primary melee weapon: power sword
* Secondary sidearm: bolt pistol

Reason:

This combination supports authority, calm pose, field capability, and readable game-character presentation without letting the weapon dominate the character.

Excluded from v1:

* Daemonhammer
* Inferno pistol
* Psycannon
* Force weapon
* Multiple pistols
* Tactical rifle
* Chainsword as default
* Book or relic tome

Weapon note:

The power sword should be sheathed or held low in the main hero render. An activated blade can be shown later only as an optional breakdown render.

## Inquisitorial Rosette Decision

The rosette is the most important Inquisitor authority symbol.

It should be designed early because it affects the chest, belt, chain, and silhouette logic.

Possible placements:

* On a chain
* On the belt
* In a small chest badge case
* Partly hidden and revealed in presentation shots

Current recommendation:

For the power armor direction, use either a chain placement or small chest badge case.

Avoid making it huge on the center chest at first.

Reason:

A controlled placement feels more believable and more mature than a superhero-style chest emblem.

Needs IP review:

Fan-art and safer original versions may require different symbol treatment.

## Servo-Skull Scope Warning

A servo-skull is lore-appropriate, but it is not part of v1.

Reason:

It becomes a second mini-character asset:

* Separate sculpt
* Separate UVs and textures
* Possible cables, lenses, parchment, or mechanical parts
* Possible rig or floating presentation setup
* Extra lighting and composition work for final renders

Decision:

No servo-skull in v1.

Possible future use:

Deferred to v2.

## Books And Relic Tome Decision

Books are not included in v1.

Reason:

The user does not want books in this version.

Additional reason:

Books increase text, inscription, and clutter risks.

Allowed optional relic options:

* No relic
* Warding reliquary
* Sealed document case or scroll tube

One maximum.

## Elements To Avoid Unless Verified

Avoid these until specifically checked:

* Fake High Gothic text
* Random Latin-looking phrases
* Invented prayers
* Random runes
* Random skull placement without design purpose
* Chaos symbols
* Space Marine chapter symbols
* Specific faction icons from unrelated Imperial groups
* Xenos technology used as decoration without explanation
* Daemonic items
* Overly clean superhero armor
* Fantasy knight armor that lacks sci-fi function
* Too many chains, seals, and relics at once
* Astartes backpack or power unit silhouette
* Space Marine chest / boot / pauldron proportions

## Text And Inscription Rule

Default decision:

No readable text until approved.

For purity seals, armor inscriptions, parchment, and document details:

* Use blank or unreadable texture detail during blockout
* Add readable text only after verification
* Do not invent Latin or religious phrases
* Do not use random copied text from online images
* Do not use official copyrighted long passages

Approved text list:

None yet.

Current recommendation:

Use no readable text in v1, or use only non-readable texture noise.

## Color Logic

Approved palette:

* Black
* Dark charcoal
* Blackened gunmetal
* Deep red
* Tarnished aged gold
* Tarnished brass
* Dark leather brown
* Parchment
* Deep red wax
* Bone or ivory only if later approved for restrained detail

Use color with meaning.

Notes:

* Purity seal wax usually reads best in deep red.
* Gold should be tarnished or worn, not bright fantasy gold.
* Red should support authority, wax seals, lining, or controlled accent detail.
* Too much bright metal will weaken the grimdark tone.

Do not make him overly colorful.

## Material Logic

Main material families:

* Mature skin
* Hair and beard groom
* Under-armor material
* Custom power armor
* Blackened gunmetal
* Tarnished gold or brass trim
* Heavy cloak cloth
* Leather
* Wax
* Parchment
* Optional chain metal
* Optional small glass or lens detail if justified

Each material should tell a story through wear, dirt, edge damage, polishing, and age.

The character should look used, maintained, field-worn, expensive, and ceremonial.

Pending visual exploration.

Approved exploration options:

* Long cloak plus front/back tabard
* Long cloak plus controlled chain arrangement

## Current Recommendation

For the strongest portfolio result, the current best direction is:

A human-scale, Puritan-in-philosophy, non-psyker Ordo Malleus Inquisitor with a close Henry Cavill-inspired older veteran face, custom Inquisitorial power armor, long cloak, hood resting behind the head or on the upper back, possible short tabard and limited controlled chains pending exploration, restrained gothic detail, a rosette or badge of authority, limited purity seals, power sword, bolt pistol, and no Space Marine silhouette.

Do not overdesign him.

The character should look expensive, dangerous, battle-used, ceremonial, disciplined, powerful, intimidating, and believable.
