# 04 Visual Design Bible

## File Purpose

This file translates the approved character concept into visual rules for design, modeling, texturing, and presentation.

The goal is to make every major visual decision testable before 3D production starts.

This file controls:

* Silhouette
* Visual hierarchy
* Costume layers
* Armor placement
* Material logic
* Color logic
* Prop limits
* Face and grooming direction
* Presentation pose
* What to avoid

This is not the final technical specification file. Polycount, UV sets, texture resolution, rigging, and engine setup will be defined later in `06_technical_specs.md`.

## Current Visual Direction

Working title:

The Black Verdict

Character type:

Human-scale, Puritan-in-philosophy Ordo Malleus Inquisitor.

Locked direction:

* Ordo: Ordo Malleus
* Philosophy: Puritan in philosophy, severe in method
* Psyker status: non-psyker
* Head presentation: bare head as primary
* Armor structure: custom human-scale Inquisitorial power armor
* Cloak: long cloak approved
* Hood: approved, resting behind the head or on the upper back
* Tabard / chain direction: pending visual exploration
* Servo-skull: no servo-skull in v1
* Presentation pose: standing authority pose
* Main visual priority: face, power armor silhouette, cloak integration, material realism

Core read:

A severe Imperial judge, daemon-hunter, and battle-worn veteran in expensive custom power armor who relies on authority, warding discipline, sanctified equipment, and controlled gothic presence instead of psychic effects.

## Three-Second Read

In the first three seconds, the viewer should understand:

1. He is human, not a Space Marine.
2. He is high-ranking and dangerous.
3. He belongs to a grimdark Imperial authority structure.
4. He is connected to daemon-hunting and forbidden-threat investigation.
5. His face, power armor, and cloak silhouette are the main hero features.

The viewer should not read him as:

* Priest
* Commissar
* Space Marine
* Space Marine without a helmet
* Tech-priest
* Fantasy knight
* Generic sci-fi soldier
* Superhero
* Cosplay costume

## Visual Hierarchy

The design must be built around this order:

1. Face and expression
2. Full-body power armor silhouette
3. Long cloak shape
4. Human-scale armor construction
5. Shoulder, chest, and boot proportions
6. Rosette and authority area
7. Power sword and bolt pistol placement
8. Optional tabard or controlled chain arrangement
9. Optional relic, if approved
10. Purity seals and small gothic details
11. Wear, age, grime, and material storytelling

Do not let small props, seals, skulls, chains, or weapons overpower the face and silhouette.

## Body And Proportion Rules

Approved body direction:

* Tall adult male
* Mid-to-late 40s apparent age
* Strong but realistic build
* Broad shoulders, but human-scale
* Athletic, not bodybuilder exaggerated
* Field-capable, not parade-only
* Silhouette powerful and tall
* Armor enhances authority but does not erase the human body

Avoid:

* Astartes proportions
* Space Marine power armor silhouette
* Astartes-style backpack or power unit silhouette
* Overbuilt torso barrel shape
* Oversized hands, boots, or pauldrons
* Tiny head / huge armor ratio
* Superhero V-shape exaggeration
* Thin fashion-model proportions
* Bulky tank silhouette

Production note:

The base body should remain believable under the cloak and armor. Armor thickness must not destroy anatomical logic.

## Head, Face, And Grooming Rules

Primary presentation:

Bare head.

Reason:

The face is the hero asset and must carry the Henry Cavill-inspired likeness direction.

Face direction:

* Close Henry Cavill-inspired facial structure
* Not a direct replica by default
* Older and more battle-worn than the usual heroic Cavill presentation
* Strong jaw and chin structure
* Heavy brow presence
* Mature facial planes
* Controlled serious expression
* Subtle scars and visible fatigue
* Slight fatigue or field wear
* Mid-to-late 40s maturity

Expression:

* Calm
* Severe
* Focused
* Controlled
* Judgmental
* Tired but unbroken

Avoid:

* Angry screaming face
* Hero smile
* Villain grin
* Wide fantasy glowing eyes
* Overly young face
* Beauty-filtered skin
* Perfect celebrity copy unless clearly fan-art study

Grooming:

* Short to medium dark hair
* Disciplined beard or short facial hair
* Groom should look maintained but field-worn
* Subtle grey is allowed if it supports age and authority

No helmet in v1.

No helmeted primary version in v1.

No wide-brimmed hat in v1.

A hood may exist as part of the cloak or upper back cloth structure, resting behind the head or on the upper back. A hooded variant may be explored later, but the primary version must show the face clearly.

Primary silhouette:

Custom human-scale power armor with long cloak.

Silhouette anchors:

* Bare head
* Face clearly visible
* Powerful human-scale armored shoulders
* No dominant high collar
* Hood resting behind the head or on the upper back
* Armored chest and torso
* Long cloak over the armor
* Possible front and back tabard, pending visual exploration
* Possible controlled chain arrangement, pending visual exploration
* Rosette / authority focal point
* Power sword and bolt pistol placement
* Controlled weapon placement
* Heavy armored boots
* Grounded stance

The power armor must create authority and intimidation.

The cloak must keep the character readable as an Inquisitor, not a Space Marine.

Avoid:

* Space Marine proportions
* Astartes-style backpack or power unit silhouette
* Oversized pauldrons
* Helmeted primary silhouette
* Wide-brimmed hat in v1
* Priest robe silhouette
* Floating decorative pieces with no attachment logic

## Costume Layer Structure

Use this layer order:

1. Body base
2. Base under-armor layer
3. Custom human-scale power armor
4. Long cloak
5. Hood resting behind the head or on the upper back
6. Optional front/back tabard, pending visual exploration
7. Optional controlled chain arrangement, pending visual exploration
8. Belt, holster, scabbard, and authority system
9. Armored gloves and boots
10. Rosette and approved authority details
11. Optional relic, if approved
12. Limited purity seals and parchment

### Base Clothing Layer

Purpose:

Support movement, fill gaps under armor, and prevent the costume from feeling hollow.

Possible elements:

* Dark fitted underlayer
* Heavy shirt or tunic-like layer
* Reinforced trousers
* Practical seams
* Subtle quilting or paneling only if useful

Avoid:

* Bright fabric
* Modern tactical-only look
* Fantasy robe base
* Overly clean superhero undersuit

### Long Cloak

Purpose:

Create the main Inquisitor silhouette and reduce Space Marine read.

Approved direction:

* Long heavy cloak over power armor
* Black exterior
* Dark red interior or lining if it supports the design
* Heavy cloth, leather-faced cloth, or waxed cloth
* Battle-used lower edge
* Ceremonial but worn
* Believable attachment to shoulders, chest, or back armor

Rules:

* Cloak must not hide the face
* Cloak must not hide the entire armor design
* Cloak must support the standing authority pose
* Cloth weight must feel real
* Attachment points must be believable

Avoid:

* Thin superhero cape
* Blanket-like loose cloth
* Priest robe silhouette
* Cloth that ignores armor shape
* Cloth clipping through sword, pistol, boots, or seals

### Custom Power Armor Chest And Torso

Purpose:

Give the character powerful Ordo Malleus authority without turning him into a Space Marine.

Rules:

* Human-scale torso proportions
* Expensive, ceremonial, battle-used armor
* Strong chest presence
* No Space Marine chest-plate proportions
* No oversized central emblem
* No Astartes-style backpack or power unit silhouette
* Armor must allow the head and face to remain dominant
* Trim must be moderate and purposeful
* Wear must follow contact and battle-use logic

Avoid:

* Astartes barrel torso
* Giant eagle-like chest emblem unless later verified and approved
* Overly clean superhero armor
* Fantasy knight breastplate language
* Random armor panels with no construction logic

### Pauldrons And Bracers

Purpose:

Support the armored authority read and balance the chest plate.

Rules:

* Shoulders can be powerful and ceremonial, but must remain human-scale
* Pauldrons must not create an Astartes silhouette
* Bracers should look integrated into power armor
* Armor must allow arm movement
* Details should support Ordo Malleus / Inquisitorial authority

Avoid:

* Astartes-sized shoulder armor
* Sharp fantasy spikes
* Overbuilt gauntlets
* Excessive skulls or chains
* Chain or skull decoration that makes the shoulders look Chaos-adjacent

### Belt And Harness System

Purpose:

This is the control center for the character.

It should carry:

* Rosette or authority object
* Sidearm holster
* Weapon attachment logic
* Small pouches
* Possible document tube or relic attachment

Rules:

* Belt must be readable from front and three-quarter view
* It must not become overloaded
* Each pouch or holster must have a purpose
* The rosette area must remain visible or intentionally revealable

Avoid:

* Random pouch spam
* Overly modern tactical vest language
* Belts with no believable support
* Hanging objects that would clip badly in game animation

### Armored Boots And Gloves

Purpose:

Ground the character and support the power armor read.

Boots:

* Heavy armored boots
* Human-scale, not Space Marine boots
* Expensive and battle-used
* Strong sole and toe structure
* Practical enough for field movement

Gloves:

* Armored gloves
* Human-scale hand proportions
* Functional finger articulation
* Controlled wear on knuckles and fingers

Avoid:

* Oversized armored boots
* Astartes boot proportions
* Fantasy knight sabatons
* Clean superhero gloves
* Hands that become too large for the human body

## Armor Zones

Approved v1 armor zones:

* Power armor chest and torso
* Pauldrons
* Bracers and armored gloves
* Thigh and knee armor
* Lower-leg armor
* Heavy armored boots
* Compact rear armor structure without backpack silhouette

Not approved for v1:

* Space Marine backpack
* Astartes-style power unit silhouette
* Full helmet
* Oversized pauldrons
* Massive armored gorget that hides the neck and jaw

* Chapter markings
* Sororitas chest armor language
* Mechanicus cog-heavy design

Armor should protect important areas but still show that he is human and mobile.

High.

Use for:

* Belt
* Gloves
* Holsters

* Pouches

Material feel:

* Worn edges

### Power Armor / Gunmetal

Use for:

* Chest and torso armor
* Pauldrons
* Bracers
* Armored gloves
* Thigh and knee armor
* Lower-leg armor
* Armored boots

Material feel:

* Dark gunmetal
* Blackened steel
* Oxidized steel
* Expensive but battle-used protective surface
* Controlled edge wear
* Moderate tarnished gold or brass trim

Avoid:

* Clean superhero armor
* Mirror-polished fantasy metal

### Tarnished Brass / Aged Gold

Use for:

* Trim
* Buckles
* Rosette detail
* Small ornament
* Possible relic casing

Rules:

* Must be aged and restrained
* Use as accent, not full armor color
* Dirt should collect in recesses

### Parchment And Wax

Use for:

* Limited purity seals
* Optional document object
* Small authority details

Rules:

* Deep red wax
* Aged parchment
* No readable text until approved
* Do not cover the whole costume with seals

### Bone Or Ivory

Use carefully for:

* Small relic detail
* Rosette accent
* One restrained skull or carved detail only if approved

Avoid:

* Skull spam
* Bone trophy look
* Chaos-adjacent trophy language

### Glass Or Lens Material

Optional use:

* Small optical detail
* Auspex-style element if later approved
* Subtle eye/cybernetic detail only if justified

Avoid:

* Bright sci-fi neon
* Overly modern tactical optics
* Magic glow by default

## Weapon Design Rules

Current status:

Weapon structure approved.

Approved structure:

* Primary melee weapon: power sword
* Secondary sidearm: bolt pistol
* No daemonhammer in v1
* No inferno pistol in v1
* No third hero weapon

Rules:

* Weapon should support authority, not become the whole character

Avoid:

* Giant fantasy hammer unless strongly controlled
* Multiple pistols
* Tactical rifle as default
* Weapon glow effects by default

## Rosette Direction

The rosette is mandatory.

Purpose:

It communicates Inquisitorial authority more clearly than extra weapons or decorations.

Preferred placement:

Belt or chain placement.

Secondary option:

Small chest badge case if it does not become a superhero emblem.

Rules:

* Must be readable in close-up
* Should not be huge on the center chest
* Can be partly hidden and revealed in presentation shots
* Must feel official, expensive, and controlled

Needs verification:

* Exact symbol language
* Exact icon shape
* Fan-art vs safer original version treatment

## Optional Signature Relic Direction

Current status:

Allowed, but not mandatory.

Best options:

* No relic
* Warding reliquary
* Sealed document case

Rules:

* One maximum
* Must support non-psyker Ordo Malleus logic
* Must not look Chaos-corrupted
* Must not introduce fake readable text
* Must be smaller than the main silhouette

Avoid:

* Multiple relics
* Glowing forbidden object
* Xenos trophy
* Daemonic artifact
* Random chained book only because it looks cool

Books are not part of v1.

Controlled chain arrangement is a visual design option, not automatically a relic.

## Purity Seal And Parchment Rules

Allowed:

* Limited purity seals
* Deep red wax
* Aged parchment strips
* Placement near armor, weapon, or relic if justified

Rules:

* Keep them restrained
* Use them to support warding and authority
* No readable text until approved
* Avoid repeating the same seal everywhere

Purity seals are approved because the user wants wax-and-parchment ribbon details, but they must remain controlled and not become full costume clutter.

Suggested maximum for v1:

* 3 to 5 visible purity seal clusters total

Avoid:

* Seal spam
* Full parchment cape
* Fake Latin
* Random copied text
* Overly clean bright parchment

## Symbol And Inscription Rules

Default decision:

No readable inscriptions yet.

Allowed for blockout:

* Blank parchment
* Non-readable texture noise
* Placeholder marked as pending verification
* Simple approved shapes only after verification

Not allowed:

* Fake High Gothic
* Fake Latin
* Invented prayers
* Random runes
* Chaos-like markings
* Space Marine chapter symbols
* Specific unrelated Imperial faction symbols

Any exact symbol must be checked before final production.

## Warding Logic For Non-Psyker Ordo Malleus

Because he is non-psyker, he should not rely on psychic effects.

Visual support should come from:

* Controlled warding seals
* Sanctified equipment
* Rosette authority
* One optional relic
* Maintained armor and weapons
* Severe expression and field wear

Risk:

This direction can easily lead to relic clutter.

Control rule:

Warding logic must be shown through a few strong, readable details, not many small random objects.

## Wear And Storytelling Rules

The character should look used, maintained, and field-experienced.

Approved wear:

* Subtle facial scars
* Skin fatigue
* Worn leather edges
* Dirt in cloak hems
* Polished metal contact points
* Scratches on armor edges
* Slight discoloration on parchment
* Wax cracks
* Tarnish in metal recesses

Avoid:

* Random blood
* Gore
* Extreme battle damage
* Cartoon villain grime
* Perfect clean showroom armor
* Mud-covered survivalist look

Storytelling principle:

Show history through wear and construction, not through excessive props.

## Presentation Pose Direction

Intended hero pose:

Standing authority pose.

Rules:

* Calm upright posture
* Face clearly visible
* Weapon sheathed or held low
* One hand may rest near belt, rosette, or sidearm
* Cloak silhouette must be readable
* Armor and belt area must be visible
* Materials must be easy to judge in final renders

Avoid:

* Running
* Attacking
* Screaming
* Superhero stance
* Weapon-over-face composition
* Pose that hides the front costume logic

## IP-Safe Visual Control

The design may be developed as fan art, but the visual bible must also support a safer original portfolio version.

Fan-art version may use:

* Clear Inquisitor direction
* Ordo Malleus framing
* Inquisition-style authority
* Lore-faithful equipment

Safer portfolio version should use:

* Original faction naming
* Original symbol design
* No exact official icon copying
* No direct Henry Cavill claim
* Same silhouette and material logic

Do not claim the character is official Games Workshop, Amazon, or Henry Cavill work.
